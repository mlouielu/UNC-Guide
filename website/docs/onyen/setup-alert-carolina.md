設定 Alert Carolina
===================

請參考: [Receive Notifications](https://alertcarolina.unc.edu/register/)


設定到你的手機號碼
------------------

:::info

如何[申請美國手機號碼](../daily/comm.md)

:::

1. 到 [Onyen Service](https://its.unc.edu/onyen-services/)
1. 選擇 **Register or Update Alert Carolina Number**
